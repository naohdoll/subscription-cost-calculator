FAQ

How do I calculate the monthly cost of a yearly subscription?

Divide the yearly price by 12. For other cycles, divide by the number of months in the cycle: 3 for quarterly, 6 for every six months, 24 for every two years. This calculator does it for you.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser (local storage), so it is still there when you come back on the same device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use "Clear all" to delete it.

Which currency does it use?

Any. Enter every price in the currency you pay in; totals are shown in the same currency.

How can I cut my subscription costs?

List everything first: bank and card statements show charges that are easy to forget. Then mark subscriptions you rarely use as Review or Cancel, check whether a yearly plan is cheaper for the ones you keep, and look for overlapping services, such as two music or cloud storage plans.
